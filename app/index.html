⚠️ Critical Missable Warning

Only one trophy can be missed: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate with Lucia on Jason's house balcony in Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth (PowerPyx).

Roadmap & Stages

Stage 1: Story + Missable

Play through the 5 chapters (60–80 hours). Complete Lucia's Chapter 1 balcony meditation ("Positive Mental Attitude"). Scan vehicles and animals as you go. Keep manual saves per chapter.

Stage 2: 100% Completion

Unlock the checklist after the story (Progress > Stats > 100% Completion). Complete 70 of 80 Challenges, scan 200/277 vehicles, study 100/255 animals. Lenient completion (~60% true completion required).

Stage 3: Strangers & Freaks

Complete the 8 required stranger mission strands (Kirk, Kyle, Yago, Pilot Pete, Randy, Kenton, Billy, Bret) and character questlines (Andy, Tyrone, etc.).

Stage 4: Cleanup & Platinum

Wrap up remaining collectibles (card sets, WAiNK scans, stunt jumps), crime trophies (bank vaults, armored trucks, gang operations), and miscellaneous feats to claim the platinum.
